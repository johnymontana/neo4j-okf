# FY2026 Cost Allocation Memo

**From:** Finance (human:jsmith@acme)
**Date:** 2026-02-01
**Status:** adopted

## Summary

Effective 2026-02-01, Acme allocates the full cost of goods sold to each order.
Gross margin is recognized revenue minus **full COGS**, where full COGS is the
sum of four components:

1. Product cost
2. Inbound fulfillment cost
3. Outbound shipping cost
4. Payment processing fees

This supersedes the product-cost-only formula the analytics team has used since
2023. The change reduces reported gross margin by roughly 4-6 percentage points
depending on category, and closes the reconciliation gap against the general
ledger.

## Why the change

The previous definition could not be reconciled to the general ledger. Three
categories in particular — bulky goods, cold chain, and marketplace returns —
carry fulfillment and shipping costs large enough that excluding them made
category-level margin comparisons meaningless.

## What this means for reporting

Any margin figure served to a decision-maker after 2026-02-01 must use full
COGS. The pre-2026 definition is retained only for reproducing historical
reports, and must be labelled as such.

Revenue recognition itself is unchanged; see the Revenue Recognition Policy.

## Review cadence

This standard is reviewed annually. Numbers produced after 2026-12-31 must be
re-verified against the FY2027 standard before they are served.
