Gross Margin

Gross margin is the headline profitability number the analytics team reports in the weekly business review. This page is the practical description the team has used since 2023.

How we calculate it

Gross margin for a period is recognized revenue minus product cost. Product cost comes from the acme.catalog.products table, joined to order lines on product_id.

gross_margin(period) = revenue(period) - product_cost(period)

Revenue itself follows the rules described on the Revenue Recognition page. Order-level inputs come from the Customer Orders table in the warehouse.

Known caveats

  • Fulfillment and outbound shipping costs are not included. Several people have asked about this; see the FY2026 Cost Allocation Memo for where Finance landed.
  • Payment processing fees are booked centrally and are not attributed to individual orders here.
  • Marketplace channel orders were backfilled in March 2024; figures before that date understate volume.

Where the number shows up

The weekly deck pulls from the Margin Daily dashboard. If the number looks wrong, start with the Margin Drop Triage runbook.
